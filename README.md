# 🛍️  Zara Revenue and Product Performance Analysis

![Zara Sales Dashboard](Zara%20Sales%20Report.png)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Zara Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to transform raw retail sales data into actionable business insights.

The goal was to analyze product performance, revenue distribution, sales volume, and product positioning — while designing a dashboard that enables users to explore the data dynamically.

Rather than focusing only on creating visuals, this project was designed around a key business question:

> **"What decisions can we make from the data?"**

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the top revenue contributors?
- Which products are underperforming?
- How is revenue distributed between men's and women's sections?
- Does product positioning influence revenue?
- Which products contribute most significantly to overall sales?
- How can decision-makers quickly identify revenue drivers?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue by Section

A comparison of total revenue generated by:

- Women's section
- Men's section

This provides a high-level view of which section contributes more to overall revenue.

### 2. Revenue by Product Position

Revenue is analyzed based on product placement within the store:

- End-cap
- Aisle
- Front of Store

This helps investigate whether product positioning is associated with higher revenue.

### 3. Revenue by Product Name

A detailed ranking of products based on total revenue.

The dashboard highlights the strongest revenue-generating products, helping identify the products that have the greatest impact on business performance.

### 4. Revenue Contribution / Waterfall Analysis

A waterfall-style visualization shows how individual products contribute toward total revenue.

This makes it easier to understand the cumulative impact of different products.

### 5. Interactive Filtering

The dashboard supports dynamic filtering and exploration, allowing users to slice the data and analyze different aspects of the business.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **Women's products generate significantly more revenue than men's products.**
- **Cashmere Blend Knit Sweater** is the highest-revenue product among the products shown.
- **Cropped Leather Jacket** and **Leather Biker Jacket** are also major revenue contributors.
- Revenue varies considerably across product names, indicating that a relatively small group of products can account for a substantial share of revenue.
- Product positioning can be analyzed to understand how store placement relates to revenue performance.

> **Note:** These observations are based on the dataset used for this portfolio project and should not be interpreted as Zara's current real-world financial performance.

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The raw dataset was prepared for analysis through data transformation and cleaning processes.

Key steps included:

1. Importing the raw retail dataset into Power BI.
2. Cleaning and transforming the data using **Power Query**.
3. Checking data types and handling inconsistencies.
4. Preparing fields required for analysis.
5. Creating calculated measures using **DAX**.
6. Building relationships and structuring the analytical model.
7. Designing interactive dashboard visuals.
8. Validating KPIs and visual outputs.
